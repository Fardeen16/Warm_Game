# Animal reference and scale

> Archived direction: on 2026-10-10 the owner changed the creature to a demon that
> lures the freezing protagonist inside. This research is retained as historical

## Wood bison

An adult bull wood bison is the best reference for this prototype. Alaska Department
of Fish and Game describes males as approximately 1.8 m at the shoulder, 3 m long,
and over 907 kg. It describes a dense coat, northern habitat and musculature adapted
to sweeping deep snow away from winter forage. Bison also seek shelter in severe weather.

Sources:
- https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=woodbison.main
- https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=woodbisonrestoration.herdupdates
- https://www.nps.gov/subjects/bison/about.htm

Body length includes head and neck; shoulder height includes legs and hump. Neither
measurement is the size of an empty body cavity. A ruminant abdomen is occupied by
viscera. The game therefore depicts displaced/cut tissue and some remaining organs,
with the player curled in the small space made available. It is an artistic reconstruction,
not a veterinary model.

## Warmth: plausible fiction, uncertain duration

A recently killed large mammal retains heat; dense hair and hide can slow its loss.
Game-meat guidance explicitly discusses retained body heat and explains that opening
the body and removing entrails speeds cooling. This supports residual heat as a story
premise. It does not establish that sheltering a person inside is practical, safe, or
effective for several hours in a blizzard.

Sources:
- https://idfg.idaho.gov/press/field-freezer-tips-processing-your-big-game-animal-0
- https://mdc.mo.gov/magazines/missouri-conservationist/2026-08/field-dressing-your-harvest
- https://idfg.idaho.gov/hunt/big-game/field-dressing

Several hours of survival remains a dramatic assumption. It would depend on how
recently the animal died, temperature, wind, exposed openings, clothing and insulation
from the ground. Blood and other moisture can wet clothing and worsen heat loss;
official cold-weather guidance emphasizes staying dry and sheltered from wind.
https://www.nps.gov/romo/planyourvisit/hypothermia.htm

## Implemented art envelope

- Protagonist: 1.78 m tall.
- Cavity: 1.90 m length, 0.85 m width, 0.72 m height at its widest point.
- Remaining tissue reduces the usable space further; knees and shoulders are tucked.
- Cropped, slanted ribs and dorsal spine suggest an animal lying on its side.
- The internal camera cannot walk or turn all the way around.
- The carcass does not move. Camera movement is the survivor's breathing.
- Exact interior dimensions are adjustable art assumptions pending review.
